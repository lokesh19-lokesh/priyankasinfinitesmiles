Biomimetic Artistry

Art, Science & Gentle Precision

Cosmetic dentistry at Priyanka's Infinite Smiles focuses on enhancing your natural beauty rather than creating artificial-looking teeth.

Whether fixing a jagged chip sustained from an accident, eliminating small gaps between front incisors, or sculpting an asymmetrical gum line, we utilize minimally invasive, biomimetic techniques that preserve maximum healthy natural enamel.

Led by Dr. Priyanka Vijayakumar, our cosmetic solutions blend nano-hybrid composite materials and lithium disilicate ceramics that perfectly match the light refraction and hue of your surrounding teeth.

Services

Our Cosmetic Dentistry Disciplines

Discover conservative, highly artistic dental solutions for every aesthetic desire.

Composite Edge Bonding

Single-visit sculpting of fractured, chipped, or uneven tooth edges using multi-layered nano-hybrid composite resin with zero enamel shaving.

Porcelain Veneers

Custom ultra-thin ceramic facings permanently bonded to front teeth to redesign tooth shape, color, and spacing with high durability.

Laser Gum Sculpting

Gentle soft-tissue diode laser contouring to fix gummy smiles or uneven gum levels painlessly with instant bloodless healing.

Laser Teeth Whitening

45-minute chairside laser teeth whitening lightening deep tea, coffee, and food stains up to 7-8 shades safely.

Protocol

The Cosmetic Consultation Process

We listen to your smile aspirations, photograph your teeth under studio lighting, and formulate a conservative treatment plan that fits your schedule and budget.

1

Smile Evaluation & Photography

High-definition macro photography and shade mapping to identify micro-imperfections.

Biomimetic Shade Matching

Selecting enamel and dentin resin shades that seamlessly match your natural tooth luster.

3

Artistic Layering & Sculpting

Incremental free-hand resin layering or precision ceramic placement under surgical dental loupes.

4

Multi-Stage High-Gloss Polish

Diamond paste polishing to create a glass-smooth surface that feels completely natural to your tongue.
