Clinical Overview

What Are Dental Implants?

The closest modern medical science has come to replicating your natural teeth from root to crown.

A dental implant is a precision-engineered screw made of medical-grade biocompatible titanium or ceramic that is placed securely into your jawbone. Over a short healing period, bone cells physically bond to the implant surface through a natural biological process known as osseointegration.

Once fused, the implant acts as a rock-solid root anchor. A custom-crafted, translucent Zirconia ceramic crown is attached via a precision abutment, seamlessly matching the shade, translucency, and contour of your adjacent teeth.

Clinical Indications

Common Dental Problems We Address

Dental implants provide a definitive, permanent solution for diverse tooth loss conditions.

Single Missing Tooth

Replaces a lost incisor, canine, or molar without shaving down or crowning healthy adjacent teeth as required with traditional bridges.

Multiple Missing Teeth

Implant-supported multi-unit bridges bridge large spans of missing teeth, providing rigid stability without bulky removable plates.

Loose & Slipping Dentures

Implant overdentures snap firmly onto titanium locators, eliminating messy denture adhesives and embarrassing slippage while eating.

Full Arch Tooth Loss

All-on-4 and All-on-6 full arch rehabilitation restores an entire upper or lower dental arch with a fixed, permanent bridge.

Surgical Roadmap

The Step-by-Step Implant Procedure

At Priyanka’s Infinite Smiles, our implant workflow is fully digitized. We eliminate guesswork through 3D virtual treatment planning and minimally invasive surgical guides.

Pain-Free Protocol
Topical micro-anesthesia ensures complete comfort
1

Stage 1: 3D CBCT Imaging & Virtual Planning

We capture high-definition 3D cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) scans to evaluate bone height, density, and nerve canals with sub-millimeter accuracy.

2

Stage 2: Guided Implant Placement

Under painless local anesthesia, the bio-compatible titanium post is placed into the exact computer-planned position. A customized healing abutment is placed.

3

Stage 3: Osseointegration (Healing Phase)

Over 8 to 12 weeks, your jawbone fuses securely to the porous implant surface. Temporary aesthetic crowns are provided for front teeth during this period.

4

Stage 4: Precision Zirconia Crown Delivery

A digital optical scan is taken to mill a monolithic, multi-layered Zirconia crown. Dr. Priyanka torques and seals the final crown with balanced bite occlusion.

Transparent Dentistry

Clinical Benefits & Considerations

We believe in complete transparency so you can make informed decisions regarding your oral healthcare.

Clinical Advantages

  • Bone Preservation: Stimulates jawbone growth and prevents sunken facial aging.
  • Natural Aesthetics: Emerge seamlessly from your gum line just like a natural tooth.
  • Healthy Adjacent Teeth: Unlike bridges, neighboring healthy teeth are untouched.
  • High Success Rate: Greater than 95-98% long-term documented clinical success rate.

Clinical Considerations

  • Healing Duration: Biological osseointegration requires adequate healing time before heavy biting.
  • Bone Volume Prerequisite: Patients with long-standing missing teeth may require bone grafting or sinus lifts.
  • Systemic Health Factors: Uncontrolled diabetes or heavy smoking may require prior medical clearance.
  • Oral Hygiene Adherence: Regular brushing, flossing, and 6-month checkups are required to prevent peri-implantitis.
