The Science of Beauty

What is Digital Smile Designing (DSD)?

Where advanced computerized facial engineering meets high-precision dental ceramics.

Digital Smile Design is a comprehensive cosmetic diagnostic tool that analyzes the relationship between your teeth, gums, lips, and facial expressions. Rather than treating teeth in isolation, Dr. Priyanka Vijayakumar evaluates your unique facial golden proportions.

Using 3D intraoral scans and high-resolution facial photography, we create a digital simulation of your future smile. You actually see and approve a temporary "mockup trial" in your mouth before any permanent ceramic veneers are fabricated.

Dental Imperfections Corrected by Smile Designing

Smile designing addresses multifaceted cosmetic concerns in as few as 2 comfortable visits.

Diastema & Tooth Gaps

Closes wide or uneven spaces between front teeth seamlessly without months of braces or bulky composite patches.

Intrinsic Enamel Staining

Masks stubborn tetracycline banding, fluorosis patches, and deep enamel discoloration unresponsive to bleaching.

Chipped, Worn or Short Teeth

Rebuilds length lost to nocturnal teeth grinding (bruxism) or accidental trauma, restoring youthful smile curvature.

Gummy or Uneven Smile Lines

Pairs with painless laser gum contouring to harmonize the pink-and-white aesthetic balance of your smile.

The Aesthetic Journey

Your 4-Step Smile Design Experience

Experience an artistic transformation tailored entirely around your desires. You retain complete creative control over the shade, shape, and length of your final veneers.

1

Visit 1: Facial Analysis & 3D Digital Scan

We capture digital photos, 3D video of your natural smile dynamics, and high-precision intraoral digital impressions. No uncomfortable gooey trays.

2

Digital Simulation & Live Mouth Mockup

We generate a 3D digital model and place a reversible resin mockup directly over your teeth. You look in the mirror and review your future smile before making any commitments.

3

Conservative Micro-Preparation

Under magnification, Dr. Priyanka gently sculpts 0.3mm to 0.5mm of outer enamel to accommodate the ceramic shells. Handcrafted temporary veneers are placed.

4

Visit 2: Adhesive Bonding & Smile Reveal

Custom lithium disilicate (E.max) veneers are try-in checked, micro-etched, and bonded using medical-grade light-cured resin cement. Bite is verified with T-scan accuracy.

Treatment Advantages

  • Stain-Resistant Glass Glaze: Highly glazed porcelain does not stain from coffee, red wine, or curry.
  • Rapid Transformation: Full aesthetic results achieved in approximately 7 to 10 days.
  • Natural Light Reflection: Translucent ceramic mimics natural tooth enamel depth.
  • Predictable Results: Live mockup preview eliminates surprises.

Clinical Considerations

  • Irreversible Process: Minimal enamel reduction is required, making the restoration permanent.
  • Night Guard for Clenchers: Patients with bruxism should wear a protective nighttime occlusal splint.
  • Severe Malocclusion: Significant bite collapse or skeletal crowding may require preliminary aligners.

Smile Designing FAQs

Most smile makeover treatments take only 2 clinical appointments over 7 to 10 days. The first visit covers digital scanning, photography, and live mockup review. The second visit delivers and bonds your final hand-crafted porcelain veneers.
