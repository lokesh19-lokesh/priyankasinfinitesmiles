Why Clear Aligners Are the Preferred Choice

Removable, nearly invisible, and custom-fabricated to fit snugly over your teeth.

Clear aligners are a modern alternative to traditional metal braces. Using digital 3D scans of your mouth, we design a series of clear, medical-grade thermoplastic trays that apply calibrated micro-forces to gently guide your teeth into optimal arch alignment.

Because they are 100% removable, you can eat all your favorite foods, brush, and floss normally without food getting trapped in metal brackets.

Clear Aligners FAQs

For optimal results, clear aligners should be worn for 20 to 22 hours per day. You only remove them when eating, drinking hot beverages, and brushing or flossing your teeth.

Treatment length varies based on the degree of crowding or bite issues. Mild to moderate alignment cases are frequently completed in 6 to 12 months, whereas complex malocclusions may take 12 to 18 months.
