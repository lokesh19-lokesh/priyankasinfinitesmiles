Why Choose In-Office Laser Whitening?

Over-the-counter strips and abrasive whitening toothpastes can wear down enamel and cause intense tooth sensitivity.

At Priyanka’s Infinite Smiles, our in-office laser bleaching is supervised directly by Dr. Priyanka Vijayakumar. We apply a protective light-cured gingival barrier that shields your sensitive gum tissue completely before applying our pH-neutral whitening gel.

Cold-spectrum laser light activates the gel to break down internal chromogen bonds inside the tooth enamel safely, lifting your smile by up to 7 to 8 shades in a single chairside visit.

The 45-Minute Protocol

How Our Whitening Procedure Works

A seamless, relaxed chairside experience designed for maximum brightness and zero discomfort.

1. Ultrasonic Polish

Surface plaque, tartar, and extrinsic biofilm are gently removed to allow uniform gel penetration.

2. Gum Isolation

A liquid dam is applied along the gum line and cured to protect delicate soft tissues from any gel contact.

3. Laser Activation

Medical whitening gel is applied and activated under cold laser light in two 15-minute cycles.

4. Enamel Shield

A calcium-phosphate remineralizing paste is applied to seal enamel tubules and prevent post-op sensitivity.
