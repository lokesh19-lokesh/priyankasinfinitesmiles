Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

At Priyanka's Infinite Smiles Advanced Dental Studio ("we", "our", or "the clinic"), accessible from https://priyankasinfinitesmiles.com/, patient privacy and clinical confidentiality are foundational to our medical practice. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal and medical information we collect and how we safeguard it.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, request a consultation, or communicate via WhatsApp/telephone, we may collect:

  • Personal contact information: Full Name, Mobile Number, Email Address.
  • Appointment preferences: Preferred treatment discipline, date, and time slot.
  • Voluntary health notes: Symptoms, dental concerns, or clinical questions you share.

2. Clinical Confidentiality & Data Protection

All patient communications and dental records are handled with strict medical confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your private contact details or healthcare information to any third-party marketing companies.

3. WhatsApp & Electronic Communications

Our website provides direct handoff options to communicate with our clinic coordination team via WhatsApp and telephone. By initiating a conversation through these links, you consent to receiving appointment confirmations and treatment information via those channels.

4. Patient Consent for Photography in Smile Gallery

All before-and-after photographs showcased in our Smile Gallery are published solely with explicit, verified written patient consent. Identifiable facial traits are safeguarded, and images are used strictly for educational and portfolio demonstration.
