Founder & Chief Dental Surgeon

Dr. Priyanka Vijayakumar

BDS, Fellowship in Cosmetic Dentistry & Oral Implantology

Dr. Priyanka Vijayakumar is an acclaimed dental surgeon and cosmetic dental specialist in Hyderabad with more than 10 years of clinical experience. She established Priyanka’s Infinite Smiles Advanced Dental Studio to bring international standards of painless oral healthcare to Manikonda.

Renowned for her empathetic chairside manner, Dr. Priyanka has extensive training in Digital Smile Design, porcelain veneer cementation, computer-guided titanium implant placement, and rotary endodontics. She takes personal care to ensure patients understand every step of their diagnosis.

Qualifications & Recognitions
  • SumanTV Healthcare Doctor Award: Recognized for exceptional clinical contributions to modern dentistry.
  • Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS): Accredited dental surgical degree.
  • Fellowship in Cosmetic Dentistry: Specialized in micro-veneers, composite aesthetics, and smile arc analysis.
  • Fellowship in Oral Implantology: Trained in computer-guided bone mapping and single-stage implants.
  • Member, Indian Dental Association (IDA): Committed to continuing medical education.
