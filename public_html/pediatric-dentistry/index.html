Parents FAQ

Pediatric Dentistry FAQs

According to the Indian Dental Association and global pediatric standards, a child's first dental checkup should occur when the first primary tooth erupts or by their first birthday. Early visits normalize the dental environment and allow us to identify early childhood caries risk.

Baby teeth are vital for speech development, chewing nutrition, and maintaining the jaw space necessary for permanent adult teeth to emerge properly. Untreated cavities in milk teeth can damage underlying permanent tooth buds and cause severe infection.
