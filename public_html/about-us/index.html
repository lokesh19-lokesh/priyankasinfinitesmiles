Our Story

A Vision for Anxiety-Free Dentistry

Founded by Dr. Priyanka Vijayakumar, our clinic was created to transform how patients perceive dental care.

Too often, dental treatment is associated with anxiety, fear of pain, cold clinical environments, and opaque treatment costs. Dr. Priyanka envisioned a warm, luxury studio atmosphere where patients are greeted by name, listened to with empathy, and given transparent, evidence-based recommendations.

Conveniently situated right beside Lanco Hills in Chitrapuri Colony, Manikonda, we serve individuals and families from Manikonda, Puppalguda, Gachibowli, Shaikpet, Jubilee Hills, and the Financial District. Over the last decade, we have transformed more than 8,500 smiles.

Hospital-Grade Safety

Our 7-Step Class-B Sterilization Protocol

Patient safety is our sacred commitment. We employ an internationally certified Class-B vacuum autoclave that sterilizes surgical instruments, dental handpieces, and burs through 7 rigorous steps:

  • Step 1: Decontamination: Ultrasonic immersion in medical-grade enzymatic disinfectant.
  • Step 2: Scrubbing & Rinsing: Manual micro-scrubbing removing all microscopic biological debris.
  • Step 3: Thermal Drying: Complete moisture elimination to prevent oxidation.
  • Step 4: Surgical Pouch Sealing: Sealed in color-changing indicator pouches.
  • Step 5: Class-B Autoclaving: Vacuum fractionated steam sterilization at 134°C under high pressure.
  • Step 6: UV Storage: Stored in sealed ultraviolet germicidal cabinets until use.
  • Step 7: Chairside Opening: Pouches are torn open directly in front of the patient.
