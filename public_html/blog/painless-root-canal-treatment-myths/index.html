Debunking Root Canal Myths: Why Modern Endodontics is Truly Painless

By Dr. Priyanka Vijayakumar February 2026 6 Min Read

Few medical terms inspire more unnecessary dread than "root canal." Yet, in modern dentistry, this procedure is straightforward, routine, and virtually pain-free.

Myth 1: Root canal treatment is painful.

The Reality: Root canals do not cause pain; they eliminate it. The agonizing throbbing sensation associated with dental infection stems from inflamed nerves inside the closed tooth pulp. By using localized computerized anesthetics and topical numbing gels, patients feel nothing more than gentle vibration during the procedure.

Myth 2: Pulling the tooth is easier and cheaper than an RCT.

The Reality: Nothing artificial can completely replace the natural resilience of your own tooth root. Extracting a tooth creates a gap that causes neighboring teeth to drift, tilts your bite, and triggers localized bone loss. Restoring that gap later with a bridge or implant is far more expensive than saving the natural tooth root with an RCT and crown.

Myth 3: Root canals require multiple painful appointments.

The Reality: Decades ago, hand files required multiple dressing visits. Today, with computerized rotary NiTi systems and electronic apex locators, Dr. Priyanka completes most root canals in a single, relaxed 50 to 60-minute visit.
