Dental Implants in Hyderabad: Procedure, Lifespan & What to Expect

By Dr. Priyanka Vijayakumar Updated: March 2026 7 Min Clinical Read

Tooth loss affects more than just aesthetics; it impacts your chewing efficiency, facial bone volume, and speech clarity. Fortunately, dental implants have become the gold standard in permanent tooth replacement.

1. What Makes Dental Implants Superior to Bridges?

Traditional dental bridges require grinding down healthy adjacent teeth on either side of the empty gap to serve as crown anchors. In contrast, a dental implant stands independently. By replacing the root of the missing tooth, it preserves the integrity of neighboring teeth and stimulates natural jawbone regeneration through mastication.

2. The 3D Digital Planning Advantage

At Priyanka's Infinite Smiles in Manikonda, every implant procedure begins with a 3D Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) scan. This enables Dr. Priyanka to assess:

  • Exact cortical and cancellous bone density
  • Proximity to the inferior alveolar nerve in the lower jaw
  • Location of the maxillary sinus cavities in the upper jaw
  • Virtual angulation for optimal crown screw emergence

3. Osseointegration: The Key to Lifelong Stability

Osseointegration is the direct biochemical fusion between living bone cells and the micro-textured titanium surface of the implant. This process typically takes between 8 and 12 weeks. Once complete, the implant can withstand chewing forces equal to—or greater than—a natural healthy tooth root.

4. Lifespan and Maintenance

When maintained with thorough brushing, flossing, or water-flossing, and 6-month preventive checkups, high-grade dental implants routinely last 25 years to a lifetime.
