What is Modern Rotary Root Canal Therapy?

Root canals do not cause pain—they relieve it permanently.

When deep dental decay or tooth cracks allow oral bacteria to reach the inner pulp chamber (containing nerves and blood vessels), it causes intense throbbing pain, sensitivity to hot and cold, and potential abscess formation.

At Priyanka’s Infinite Smiles, Dr. Priyanka Vijayakumar and our endodontic team utilize flexible nickel-titanium (NiTi) rotary files, high-precision electronic apex locators, and ultrasonic disinfection to thoroughly clean and disinfect the root canals in a single comfortable visit.

Clinical Stages

The Single-Sitting Procedure

We take pride in performing precise, sterile endodontics that eliminates the need for multiple painful dressings or repeated visits.

1

Digital Diagnosis & Painless Numbing

Low-dose digital RVG x-rays locate canal anatomy. Gentle computerized micro-anesthesia numbs the tooth completely before we start.

2

Rubber Dam Isolation & Canal Access

A rubber dam isolator protects the tooth from saliva, preventing recontamination during treatment.

3

Automated Rotary Cleaning & Irrigation

Microscopic NiTi rotary files and warm ultrasonic antibacterial irrigants clean out infected pulp along the entire root canal length.

4

3D Hermetic Obturation & Crown Prep

The sterile canals are sealed permanently with biocompatible warm gutta-percha. A tooth-colored Zirconia crown is placed to prevent future fractures.
